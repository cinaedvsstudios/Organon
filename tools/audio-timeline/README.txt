ORGANON — ADVANCED AUDIO TIMELINE v0.13

Open advanced.html through Organon or in a modern Chromium-based browser. Extract the whole package before opening it; advanced.html requires the bundled js folder.

Project Files
- Click Import, Browse a folder, or drop supported media into the app / black Media Bin.
- Files stay in Project Files until dragged onto the timeline.

Canvas dimensions
- Set W and H in Project Controls and press ✓ or Enter.
- The compositor, PNG snapshots, and preview aspect ratio use that selected size.

Timeline
- Drag a pill to reposition it. Drag the right end to trim/extend it.
- Video keeps embedded audio by default. Right-click a video to extract a separate audio clip only when needed.
