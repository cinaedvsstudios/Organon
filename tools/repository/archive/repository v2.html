📐 Interface Architecture & Color Token Blueprint

Naming conventions, design tokens, and color contrast rules for modern dark-themed web interfaces.

🏷️ CLASSIFICATION & NOMENCLATURE

1. Popover vs. Tooltip vs. Modal

* Tooltip: Tiny, non-interactive, hover-triggered inline label showing simple descriptors (e.g. "Save layout").
* Popover: Multi-line rich content cards positioned relative to anchors containing options or lists.
* Modal: Viewport-blocking central dialogs requiring dismissal actions.

2. Drawer vs. Sheet

* Drawer: Side menu slider panels anchored to the left/right boundaries.
* Sheet: Bottom-anchored layouts designed for mobile interactions.

🎨 CONTRAST GUIDELINES & WCAG AA

To maintain strict WCAG AA compliance, text components must maintain a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against adjacent background blocks. Large header strings require a minimum ratio of 3:1. This ensures legibility for users with visual impairments.
